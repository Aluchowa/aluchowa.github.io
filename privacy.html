Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Home Dashboard is a private, self-hosted app used by a single household to show and control its own smart-home devices. This page explains what it does with data from Google.

What it accesses

When a member of the household signs in with Google, the app asks for permission to use the Google Nest Smart Device Management API. With that permission it reads the household's own Nest thermostats and cameras (temperatures, heating settings, camera and doorbell events, live video when someone asks to view it) and changes thermostat settings when someone in the home asks it to.

Where the data goes

Everything stays on the household's own computer at home. The sign-in token and device information are stored only there. Nothing is sent to the app's author, sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising, analytics or training. Camera video is streamed to the household's own screens and is not recorded by the app.

Keeping and deleting

The app keeps only what it needs to work (the sign-in token and the latest device readings). Disconnecting Nest in the app's settings deletes the token. Access can also be removed at any time from your Google Account: Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services → Home Dashboard.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Home Dashboard's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Questions

This is a personal project. Questions can be raised through the project's GitHub page.
