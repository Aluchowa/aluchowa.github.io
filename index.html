Home Dashboard

A private dashboard for one household.

Home Dashboard is a small, self-hosted app that runs on a computer inside a family home. It shows and controls that household's own lights, speakers, heating and cameras on a wall tablet and the family's phones.

It is not a public service: there are no accounts to sign up for, and it is only used by the people who live in the home. Its "Sign in with Google" is used solely so the household can connect its own Google Nest devices.
